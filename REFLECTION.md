# Reflection — synodos

## Decisions

The best decision I made in this project was the idea itself. As someone who struggles with the idea of building a portfolio outside of college and building a meaningful career, with the advancements of AI, it can be daunting. I think the idea is cool because it gives people the opportunity to build meaningful experience while also having a reference source for future employers. My theme also is just really good looking in my opinion, the blueprint texture background and the color scheme match a Greek theme. I think pushing myself to using something like cursor also was interesting because I was surprised by how much I actually enjoyed it. It took a tiny bit to learn but I found myself finding out how to tinker with and improve my agents.

---

## What worked

I would say my overall theme and design is what I'm most satisfied with. The website follows the principles of UCD and everything works while also making sense. The project itself is a really big scope of course, but I wanted to aim for something really difficult while using an AI tool like cursor. I also am really proud of the backend work that I put into this and was also able to learn. I used new software and technologies like Supabase and Postgres.

---

## What you would do differently

I would have a better idea of what technology I wanted to use. I used very minimalistic technology at first and I ended up regretting that the more I went on with my project. This became an issue because the more I worked on it the more additions I wanted to make. When I did switch technology I ended up running into a lot of problems. I worked them all out but it was incredibly time-consuming troubleshooting even with the help of AI. When I changed databases I ended up spending literal days on how to fix my image upload for avatars and it was something I did not get done until the very last minute despite spending weekends on it and my hours after work as silly as it sounds.

---

## What you learned

I learned that I love doing this and that I am capable of doing this. Another thing I really enjoyed was working with AI and how much I could actually get done with this tool. Making this project left me with a feeling of what's next; I'm already thinking about the next projects I want to make while also perfecting Synodos. I actually plan on using cursor for future projects as well instead of VS code. Another thing I learned was how to fix things after I broke them. Sometimes it felt like I was constantly running into a wall and I didn't think I would make it by the deadline but I figured it out. A lot of it was because I started using new tech half way through and I was learning on the fly. This class has been my favorite class I've taken my whole college career because it gave me creative freedom and hands on work rather than just vocab, quizzes, tests, etc.
