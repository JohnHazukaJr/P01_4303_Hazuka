# Reflection — synodos

## Decisions

The best decision I made in this project was the idea itself. As someone who struggles with the idea of building a portfolio outside of college and building a meaningful career, with the advancements of AI, it can be daunting. I think the idea is cool because it gives people the opportunity to build meaningful experience while also having a reference source for future employers. My theme also is just really good looking in my opinion, the blueprint texture background and the color scheme match a Greek theme.

---

## What worked

I would say my overall theme and design is what I'm most satisfied with. I also am really proud of the backend work that I put into this and was also able to learn. I used new software and technologies like Supabase and Postgres.

---

## What you would do differently

I would have a better idea of what technology I wanted to use. I used very minimalistic technology at first and I ended up regretting that the more I went on with my project. This became an issue because the more I worked on it the more additions I wanted to make. When I did switch technology I ended up running into a lot of problems. I worked them all out but it was incredibly time-consuming troubleshooting even with the help of AI.

---

## What you learned

I learned that I love doing this and that I am capable of doing this. Another thing I really enjoyed was working with AI and how much I could actually get done with this tool. Making this project left me with a feeling of what's next; I'm already thinking about the next projects I want to make while also perfecting Synodos.
